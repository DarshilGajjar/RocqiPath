# Code of conduct

## Our standard

RocqiPath's issue tracker and discussions exist so that people working on
difficult problems in computational pathology can help each other. Everyone
participating is expected to make that easier rather than harder.

**Expected:**

- Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith. Most confusing
  bug reports come from someone under time pressure, not someone careless.
- Be specific. "Alignment fails on 80x TMA slides scanned on a Hamamatsu"
  helps; "it's broken" does not.
- Accept that maintainer time is finite and unpaid. A feature not being built
  is not a slight.
- Respect the data rules in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). They protect patients,
  not the project.

**Not acceptable:**

- Personal attacks, demeaning comments, or sustained disruption.
- Harassment of any kind, including unwanted contact after being asked to stop.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Posting patient-identifying material, whether careless or deliberate.
- Discrimination on the basis of age, disability, ethnicity, sex
  characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level,
  nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Scope

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and
commit history, and to any public communication where someone is representing
the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem through GitHub's
[private reporting](https://github.com/DarshilGajjar/RocqiPath/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting the maintainer directly through their GitHub profile. Reports
are handled confidentially.

Responses escalate as needed: a private clarification, a public correction,
removal of the offending content, or a block from the repository. Content that
exposes patient information is removed immediately and without discussion,
before any other step.
